HOW THE LEAGUE WORKS

Four stages, from registration to the final. Pick a stage to see what happens.
01
Register on the event page

Every club member signs up from this page. You will also find the rules, the schedule, the competition format and the current standings here.

how_to_reg MEMBER REGISTRATION
02
Collect points, climb the table

All registered members take part in challenges and earn points. Your results decide your position in the overall league table.

leaderboard LEAGUE TABLE RANKING
03
The best 16 go knockout

The 16 highest-ranked members qualify. You face an opponent in each round, and the winners advance until one participant wins the final.

• 16 Round of 16
• 8 Quarter-finals
• 4 Semi-finals
• 2 Finalists
• 1 Champion

RULES

CLEAR TOURNAMENT REGULATIONS & ENFORCEMENT

A website can detect some page-leave events, but it cannot prove nobody used another device. So we are defining clear rules instead of relying on detection alone.

tab_close RULE 01 // FOCUS BOUNDS

Stay in the arena

Leaving the challenge page may cost you the round. The final penalty (warning first, or direct disqualification) will be announced before the event.

FOCUS MONITOR ACTIVE
wifi_off RULE 02 // FAULT TOLERANCE

Technical problems

Accidental refreshes, browser crashes and network drops will have a clear recovery rule, so a glitch does not decide the result.

SESSION RECOVERY PROTOCOL
travel_explore RULE 03 // SEARCH BOUNDS

Web search

Searching is allowed only in research challenges. Each challenge states its own rule up front so expectations remain transparent.
